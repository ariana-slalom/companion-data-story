# Project Context — The Support Side

## What This Project Is
An interactive data story about the companion experience in chronic illness.
The audience is not the patient — it's the person beside them. Partners,
parents, siblings, close friends who have reorganized their lives around
someone else's diagnosis.

Built for Protogen P302 (Interactive Data Story) certification at Slalom.

## Core Belief
Healthcare systems treat companions as secondary by design. Clinical priority
must go to the unwell person. But the companion is doing an enormous, largely
invisible job — and almost no tools exist for them. This project makes that
experience visible, validates what companions carry, and closes with an
interactive prototype of what a companion-focused tool could look like.

## The User
Primary: A companion or caregiver — someone actively supporting a person with
chronic, episodic, or invisible illness. They may be in denial, over-supporting,
under-informed, or burning out. They are not looking for clinical information.
They are looking for something that sees them.

Secondary: Healthcare designers and advocates who build tools in this space
and need to understand the companion gap firsthand.

## Conditions Covered
The story applies broadly. The condition filter surfaces condition-specific
stats for: Lupus (SLE), MS, Rheumatoid Arthritis, Hashimoto's, Crohn's,
Sjögren's, EDS/HSD, and Type 1 Diabetes (including parent-of-child experience).

## Story Arc
1. Hook — gender disparity and diagnosis delay stats. Sharp and immediate.
2. The Diagnosis Journey — named, not fixed. Management not cure.
3. The Sliding Scale — severity shifts by day, by flare, by year.
4. The Pivot — who else is in the room? Day in the Life toggle.
5. The Invisible Data — caregiver burden stats. The research exists.
6. Right-Sizing Support — the companion psychological arc.
7. The Gap + Prototype — interactive mockup of a companion tool.

## Key Design Decisions
- One emotional narrative scroll — not tabs, not sections with jump nav
- Condition filter updates 3–4 stats only — not separate data models
- Day in the Life toggle is condition-agnostic — same emotional arc across all
- Closing prototype is tappable but clearly framed as illustrative, not live
- No stock photography — illustration and typography only
- Disclaimer present on entry and in footer — educational resource only

## What This Is Not
- A clinical tool
- A patient-facing app
- A data engineering exercise
- A replacement for professional medical or psychological support
